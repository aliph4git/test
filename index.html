🏛️ Borobudur Temple

Borobudur Temple is a famous Buddhist temple in Central Java, Indonesia. It is one of the largest Buddhist temples in the world. The temple has many stupas, stone carvings, and beautiful reliefs. It has several levels and a large main structure. The temple is surrounded by green landscapes and beautiful hills. Borobudur is an important historical and cultural site. Many local and international visitors come to see its beauty and learn about its history.
